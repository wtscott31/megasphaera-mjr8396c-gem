# Scientific interpretation and limitations

- **Taxonomy and provenance:** Original NCBI assembly GCA_001546855.1 is a vaginal isolate. Separate experimental evidence of gut colonization is absent; gut-like medium and *B. adolescentis* partnership are hypothetical scenarios.
- **Model:** MEMOTE applies to the original model; stoichiometric model-quality scoring alone does not validate product rates. The sequence-supported curation changes gene-level associations and should not be presented as an independently validated alternative network.
- **Biomass:** BOFdat-derived sequence DNA coefficients and related-strain protein composition are alternative assumptions; full target-strain biomass composition, GAM and NGAM remain unmeasured.
- **Thermodynamics:** Accepted native loopless FVA endpoints show loop-free feasible flux states, not thermodynamic feasibility calibrated by intracellular activities or metabolite concentrations.
- **Community:** MICOM with custom documented QP optimization includes 1,920 additional cases and 4,320 exchange-FVA ranges. A co-culture pFBA point does not prove transfer dependence; the paired blocked-transfer experiments do not support obligatory lactate cross-feeding.
- **Strain design:** Nucleotide-associated reaction-level targets cannot be called viable gene knockouts when shared GPRs predict no growth after whole-gene deletion.
- **Comparator models:** Uniquely gap-filled models and AGORA/iME375 diagnostic correction candidates must not be described as the published reference network or as experimental validation.

Do not infer human-health effects, clinical applications, fiber responses or a causal obesity link from this modeling-only manuscript.
